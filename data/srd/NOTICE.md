# SRD data

`2014/en` and `2024/en` are copied from [5e-bits/5e-database](https://github.com/5e-bits/5e-database)
at commit `bce51b3958573819e3b842fbc0cd9524fe4bc2e1` (MIT, see `LICENSE-5e-database.md`).
`2014/ru-seed` is the partial Russian translation shipped by the same project.

`i18n/ru.tm.jsonl` is a machine translation produced by `tools/srd-translate/translate.mjs`.
Lines carrying `"warn"` were accepted after failing an automatic check and need a human look.
